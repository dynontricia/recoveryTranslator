Privacy Policy

Recovery Translator is a free tool built to support recovery communities. We take your privacy seriously.

Audio & Transcripts

Audio is streamed directly from the session leader's device to OpenAI's transcription and translation APIs for real-time processing. Recovery Translator does not store, record, or retain any audio or transcript data on its servers.

OpenAI Data Policy

Audio and transcripts are processed by OpenAI and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring purposes. This monitoring is automated. Recovery Translator's use case — spoken language captioning and interpretation for recovery meetings — does not fall into any prohibited category.

If no abuse is detected by OpenAI's automated systems, no human at OpenAI will review your audio or transcripts.

OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. For more information, visit openai.com/privacy.

Session Data

Session codes and connection data exist only in memory for the duration of your session and are deleted when the session ends. Your browser stores your session code locally so you can reconnect automatically if you briefly lose connection — this is cleared when the session ends.

No Accounts, No Tracking

Recovery Translator does not require accounts, use cookies, or track users.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Session Leaders

  1. Tap I am a session leader, enter your access code and OpenAI API key.
  2. Choose how people are speaking into the session — across the room (Owl, conference mic, speakerphone) or close to the device (headset, hand-held, podium mic) — and tap Start session.
  3. Choose Full translation if you need a live Spanish translation, or English captions only for an English-only meeting that just wants live captions.
  4. Share the QR code or session code. Optionally open the big display screen for a room projector, or the captions-only pop-out window for a simpler view.
  5. Speak normally — English is captioned live as you talk.
  6. Full translation only: when a Spanish speaker will address the room, tap Spanish speaker (switch), or Approve their request in the queue. The English box then shows a live English translation of their Spanish, and Spanish attendees see a reminder to listen to the room. Tap the toggle again when they're done.
  7. Tap End session when the meeting is over.

Listeners

  1. Scan the QR code or tap I am a listener, enter the session code, choose your language (Spanish only appears for sessions that support it), and tap Join.
  2. Captions appear automatically. Spanish attendees can tap 🔊 Hear translation to also listen to the translated audio.
  3. Spanish attendees can tap 🎙 Request to speak to ask to address the room.
  4. When a Spanish speaker has the floor, Spanish screens pause captions and show a reminder to listen to the room.
  5. If you switch apps or lock your phone briefly, reopening the page should reconnect you automatically.

About Recovery Translator

Recovery Translator provides real-time spoken language interpretation and live captioning for recovery meetings, area committees, and service assemblies.

How It Works

The leader opens a session, chooses whether they need Spanish translation or just English captions, and shares a QR code. English speech is captioned live for the room the whole time. In full-translation sessions, Spanish-speaking attendees also get a continuous live Spanish translation. When a Spanish speaker addresses the room, the leader switches modes (or approves their request), and the room sees a live English translation of their words until the leader switches back.

Two Session Types

Full translation runs English captions and a live Spanish translation side by side. English captions only is a lighter option for English-only meetings that still want live, accurate captions — useful for anyone hard of hearing.

Getting Access

Leaders need an access code and an OpenAI API key. Listeners only need the session code. To request an access code, contact dynontricia@gmail.com.

Cost

Recovery Translator uses OpenAI's real-time transcription and translation APIs, billed to the leader's OpenAI account. Full-translation sessions run more concurrent connections than captions-only sessions. Please check OpenAI's current realtime API pricing for an up-to-date estimate.
