Privacy Policy

Recovery Translator is a free tool built to support recovery communities. We take your privacy seriously.

Audio & Transcripts

Audio is streamed directly from your device to OpenAI's translation API for real-time processing. Recovery Translator does not store, record, or retain any audio or transcript data on its servers.

OpenAI Data Policy

Audio and transcripts are processed by OpenAI and may be retained by OpenAI for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring purposes. This monitoring is automated and is designed to detect violations of OpenAI's usage policies — for example, using the API to generate harmful content, facilitate illegal activity, or circumvent safety systems. Recovery Translator's use case — spoken language interpretation for recovery meetings — does not fall into any of these categories.

If no abuse is detected by OpenAI's automated systems, no human at OpenAI will review your audio or transcripts. Your session content is not read, listened to, or accessed by any person.

OpenAI does not use API data to train its models. For more information, visit openai.com/privacy.

Session Data

Session codes and connection data exist only in memory for the duration of your session and are deleted when the session ends.

No Accounts, No Tracking

Recovery Translator does not require accounts, does not use cookies, and does not track users in any way.

Step-by-Step Instructions

Session Leaders

  1. Open recoverytranslator.com and tap I am a session leader.
  2. Enter your access code, your OpenAI API key, and select the primary spoken language. Tap Start session.
  3. Share the QR code or session code with your attendees.
  4. Speak normally — captions and translation are live.
  5. When a listener requests to speak, tap Approve or Deny on your screen.
  6. When the approved speaker is finished, tap Resume main session.
  7. When the meeting ends, tap End session.

Listeners — Primary Language

  1. Scan the QR code or go to recoverytranslator.com and tap I am a listener.
  2. Enter the session code, select your language, and tap Join.
  3. Captions will appear automatically as people speak.
  4. To address the group, tap Request to speak and wait for approval.
  5. When approved, speak clearly. Tap Done speaking when finished.

Listeners — Second Language

  1. Scan the QR code or go to recoverytranslator.com and tap I am a listener.
  2. Enter the session code, select Español, and tap Join.
  3. Translated captions will appear automatically as people speak.
  4. To address the group, tap Request to speak and wait for approval.
  5. When approved, speak clearly into your phone's microphone. Tap Done speaking when finished.

About Recovery Translator

Recovery Translator provides real-time spoken language interpretation for recovery meetings, area committees, and service assemblies — making meetings accessible to everyone regardless of the language they speak.

How It Works

The session leader opens a session from their device and shares a QR code with attendees. As the leader speaks, their words are transcribed and translated in real time. Attendees read captions on their phones in their preferred language. No special equipment is needed — just a phone and a browser.

Listeners who speak a different language can also request to speak. The session leader approves the request, and the system automatically translates their words back to the room's primary language.

Getting Access

Session leaders need an access code and an OpenAI API key to start a session. Listeners only need the session code provided by the leader — no account or API key required.

To request an access code, contact us at your@email.com.

Estimated Cost

Recovery Translator uses OpenAI's real-time translation API, billed directly to the session leader's OpenAI account. The estimated cost is about $0.05 per minute of active session time — typically under $5 for a two-hour meeting.

Built for Recovery

This tool was built by and for the recovery community, with anonymity and accessibility in mind. It is free to use and open source.
