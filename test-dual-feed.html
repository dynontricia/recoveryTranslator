Dual-Feed Architecture Test

Tests whether two always-on translate sessions can each reliably output their own language, regardless of what's spoken — which would remove the need for a manual Spanish-speaker toggle entirely.
What to do: press Start, then speak English for ~15 seconds, pause, then speak Spanish for ~15 seconds.

What we're testing: the critical question is the same-language passthrough — does the English feed still produce English text while you're speaking English (not translating anything)? And does the Spanish feed produce Spanish while you speak Spanish?

If both boxes stay populated no matter which language you speak, this architecture works and the toggle can go away. If a box goes silent or garbled when you speak its own language, same-language passthrough is unreliable and we keep the toggle.
